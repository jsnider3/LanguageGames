# Military sci-fi art update

The game uses original heavy-infantry armor, industrial fortifications, weathered surfaces, blue shield effects, warm muzzle flashes, and overcast blue-gray lighting. Gears of War and Halo were directional references for the military sci-fi tone; models and textures are original project assets.

## Generated material assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and copied into the project. These textures are loaded onto 3D surfaces, not used as screenshots of gameplay.

### public/textures/armor-steel.png

Final prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable PBR base-color texture for a realistic military science-fiction FPS game. Create a square flat orthographic texture of weathered dark graphite painted steel, like the armored hull of a heavy futuristic troop carrier. Dense fine surface grain, abrasion, small scratches exposing steel, subtle grime and restrained brown rust spotting. Mostly dark neutral gray with slight blue undertone, even midtone detail, no large dramatic contrast. The entire image is ONLY the uniform material surface. Flat diffuse lighting, no baked directional light, no reflections, no perspective. Seamlessly tileable on all four edges. No objects, panels, seams, borders, text, insignias, logos, icons or watermark. 1024x1024.
